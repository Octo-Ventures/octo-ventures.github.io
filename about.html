About Octo Ventures

Senior Technology Leadership, Built to Last

Who I Am

Octo Ventures is an independent technology and cybersecurity consultancy. I partner with resource-constrained organizations to navigate complex technology decisions, bringing the CIO/CTO-caliber leadership they need without the cost of a full-time executive hire.

With deep expertise spanning executive leadership, cloud architecture, AI and data science, and secure development practices, I provide strategic guidance and hands-on implementation support across every stage of your technology journey.

My Mission

I believe great technology is a strategic asset, not a cost center. My mission is to help organizations align technology investments with business strategy, navigate the complexities of technology change, and leave every system more mature, secure, and resilient than I found it.

Why an Octopus?

The octopus isn't just a name pun. Octopuses are among the most cognitively sophisticated invertebrates: intelligent, adaptive, and able to coordinate many arms toward a single outcome. That's the model for how I work, one accountable expert covering security, AI, cloud, and leadership, all coordinated toward your goals. They're also regenerative and hard to pin down, which is the resilience promise behind everything I build.
